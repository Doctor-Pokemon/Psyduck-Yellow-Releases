# Pokémon Psyduck Yellow — Releases

Downloads for **Pokémon Psyduck Yellow**, an unofficial fan project that rebuilds Pokémon Yellow's Kanto campaign inside Pokémon HeartGold (starting Psyduck / Togepi / Eevee, and more).

> ## ⚠️ BETA — FOR TESTING ONLY
> The newest release (**ALPHA 0.1**) is a pre-release, still in the late stages of testing, but so far it seems to be complete. *All features may not be in final version.* If you run into any problem, please let us know by submitting an official Issue in the Issues section of this repository: https://github.com/Doctor-Pokemon/Psyduck-Yellow-Releases/issues
> The **Kanto campaign (Pallet Town to your first Hall of Fame)** is built, and **more of the Johto postgame** is in,
> but **Johto is unfinished and has not been played through.** Expect bugs, including softlocks.

## The game in plain words
Psyduck Yellow retells **Pokémon Yellow's Kanto story inside Pokémon HeartGold**. You start with **Psyduck, Togepi or Eevee**; and Kanto's towns, gyms, Team Rocket plots, Elite Four and Champion Blue follow Yellow's teams and dialogue. The goal of this project is a finished Kanto adventure from Pallet Town to the first Hall of Fame; Johto (HeartGold's own region) is meant to come later as a postgame.

## Where it stands right now (ALPHA 0.1)
- **Built:** Pallet Town through Vermilion, Pokémon Tower, Celadon and the Rocket Hideout, Routes 8 and 11–18, Fuchsia, Saffron (with Silph Co.), Seafoam and Viridian gyms, Route 22, Victory Road, the Indigo Plateau League and the Hall of Fame.
- **Played by the developer:** New Game through Lavender Town on earlier builds, and the Kanto league and first Hall of Fame on hardware. Most of what is new since 0.2.1.0 has only had small emulator tests on prepared saves.
- **New in 0050:** your starter Eevee can evolve into all eight Eeveelutions and the new Stellar-type **Novareon** (use a Sandwich); plus fixes for the starters' evolution screen and summary sprites.
- **New in 0056:** the Pokédex opens on Kanto (1-151) with real National numbers for the starter Pokémon; TM34 is Moonblast and TM58 is Dazzling Gleam.
- **New in 0081:** Novareon's own Pokédex entry and follower, Champion Blue, item balls in Silph Co., the Tower, the Rocket Hideout and Victory Road, Moltres, and fixes for trainer teams, the Game Corner and the Silph exit.
- **New in 0105:** Pokémon Tower, Mt. Moon, Rock Tunnel, Rocket Hideout, Silph Co., the Pokémon Mansion, Viridian Gym, Victory Road and the Safari Zone rebuilt from Yellow's maps with their puzzles; a black-screen fix in Vermilion's Pokémon Center; a Pokédex registration fix.
- **New in 0110:** the VS banners name the Elite Four and Champion Blue, Strength works with Kanto badges, Victory Road has real boulders, visible switches and Yellow's first-switch puzzle on 2F, and Novareon counts on the Trainer Card.
- **New in ALPHA 0.1:** Legendary Pokémon can now be obtained. After your first Hall of Fame, Celebi waits at the Ilex Forest shrine, Regirock in Union Cave, Regice on the deepest floor of the Ice Path, Registeel in Mt. Mortar, Uxie outside the Ruins of Alph, Mesprit at the Lake of Rage and Azelf in Union Cave, each once you own enough Johto badges and only while that species is not yet registered as caught. Mr. Pokémon now gives both the Blue Orb and the Red Orb. With the Red Orb in your bag, the orb in the Embedded Tower (the chamber behind the lower door on Route 47) summons one legendary Pokémon per day (a new one each weekday): it appears as its own follower sprite, walks up to you, and the battle starts when it touches you. Mew and Arceus can only appear once nearly all other Pokémon are registered as caught. Added a Card Album key item with Gym Leader and Elite Four cards (Kanto and Johto) in three rarities. The card texts are placeholders. Lorelei and Agatha have their own battle and overworld sprites, and the Kanto Elite Four speak their Pokémon Yellow lines. Fixed a freeze: the battle against Lorelei (and the one against Agatha) in the Kanto Elite Four never started on emulators and crashed on a 3DS. Fixed the abilities of the new Eeveelutions and of Novareon: they showed impossible ability numbers and the wrong second and hidden abilities; they now have their intended abilities. The Jade Orb can be obtained again: after you have caught both Kyogre and Groudon in the Embedded Tower, Professor Oak gives it to you, and it now opens the way to Rayquaza. The orb in the Embedded Tower now works in all three chambers. The player's PC Item Storage now starts empty on a New Game (no starting items, evolution items or charms), and Fly can no longer be used until you have the Thunder Badge (the Fly HM still comes from Lt. Surge). Trainers' Pokémon no longer come out of Cherish Balls, and the Johto Gym Leaders no longer mention badges unlocking HM field moves (the HMs now come from the Kanto leaders).
- **New in 0210:** Added Hisuian Forms to Form Trader NPC.
- **New in 0200:** Johto postgame, further along: the first Johto scenes (Elm's lab, the New Bark Town and Route 29 gate scenes) were repaired; a Johto Gym Leader is always in the gym until you have beaten him, and the postgame weekday schedule applies afterwards; Johto Gym Leaders' teams scale with your Johto badges; the S.S. Aqua ferry and its lost-granddaughter quest follow HeartGold's own flow. HMs from the Kanto gyms: Brock gives Rock Smash, Misty Waterfall, Surge Fly, Blaine Rock Climb and Giovanni Whirlpool; the S.S. Anne captain gives Cut; Sabrina still gives Strength and Janine Surf. Each leader says what the HM does in the field and which Badge unlocks it. Silph Co. Field Kit: after the Master Ball the Silph Co. President also gives you a Field Kit. With the right Badge it lets you clear Cut trees, Rock Smash rocks and Strength boulders without a Pokémon that knows the move. Bag descriptions: items show real descriptions again (HeartGold's own text for the original items, new text for 52 of the added items). National Dex after your first Hall of Fame: Kanto and Johto then use a second set of wild Pokémon tables. Hundreds of species the game never handed out now turn up in the rarer slots of grass, caves and water (legendary-class Pokémon are held back for later events). Before the first Hall of Fame nothing changes. Evolutions: the Linking Cord now evolves every Pokémon that evolves by trade. Spritzee needs a held Sachet and Swirlix a held Whipped Dream, as in the games, and Kubfu can use its Scrolls. Pokémon League shop: after your first Hall of Fame the clerk in the Pokémon League lobby also sells the evolution items nothing else gives out (the stones, Linking Cord, Whipped Dream, Sachet, Apples, Pots, Galarica items, Black Augurite, Peat Block, Scrolls, Armors and Metal Alloy). Form Trader: a new NPC in the Johto Safari Zone Gate swaps a Pokémon for its Alolan or Galarian form (34 forms). Its stats, ability and moves follow the new form. The swap is one way. 3DS: a red-screen crash right before Morty's battle was worked around (confirmed on a real 3DS).
- **New in 0169:** Strength (from Sabrina, with the Marsh Badge) and Surf (from Janine) can be obtained; Officer Jenny closes the Route 15 and Route 18 gatehouses to Fuchsia until the Marsh Badge; the Warden's granddaughter gives his Gold Teeth and he rewards you with Nuggets; a sleeping Snorlax on Route 12 wakes to the Poké Flute (now a real key item); the Game Corner slot machines say they are out of order; the Running Shoes and Silph Scope have real icons.\n- **New in 0168:** a Route 7 / Route 8 shortcut opens the way from Lavender Town to Celadon; the Lavender Pokémon Tower door and sign follow Yellow; Brock's escort in Pewter is faster; both Mart clerks sell; the custom TMs show their TM numbers; the Vermilion Gym sign reads "The Lightning Unovan"; the Celadon Game Corner can be entered from the street; the Rocket Hideout spinner tiles show arrows; the Saffron Fighting Dojo master can be fought for a Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan.
- **New in 0161:** a New Game starts with the Kanto Pokédex (no National switch); Oak upgrades it to the National Pokédex later in the Johto postgame.
- **New in 0160:** replaces 0158 (and 0157): the Town Map and Fly map stay Kanto-only until Elm's call even if the game already set New Bark Town's Fly point, and walking into Pallet Town no longer unlocks it.
- **New in 0158:** replaces 0157, which crashed in battle (a Town Map change had overwritten a battle routine); same features as 0157, fixed.
- **New in 0157:** the redesigned Eevee/Psyduck starter line (nine new Eeveelutions, Stellareon), the Town Map that shows only Kanto until you reach Johto, the first parts of the Johto postgame (S.S. Aqua, Elm, Mr. Pokémon), Johto difficulty that scales with your Johto badges, the Johto League and Red, shared Kanto Mart stock, a Rare Candy move-learning fix, ladder and warp art in the caves, and 3DS crash fixes.
- **Not built yet:** elevator floor menus (stairs for now), some stand-in textures, some Game Corner and Department Store extras, and **most of Johto** (only the postgame steps above exist, and they have not been played through).


## Play it
1. Open the latest page from the [Releases](../../releases) section (or the [hosted ALPHA 0.1 patching page](https://raw.githack.com/Doctor-Pokemon/Psyduck-Yellow-Releases/main/prototype-alpha01d/index.html)).
2. Select your **own, original, unmodified USA HeartGold `.nds`**.
3. Click *Create my playable NDS*, wait for verification, then download it and load it in your DS emulator.
4. Start a **New Game**. Saves from older prototypes are not supported.

Nothing is uploaded: the patching runs in your browser and every stage is hash-checked.

## What is (and isn't) here
- Patches, patching pages and notes only. **No ROMs, saves or game files are distributed.** You must supply your own legally obtained game.
- Releases are experimental prototypes. Each release's notes say what has and has not been played.
