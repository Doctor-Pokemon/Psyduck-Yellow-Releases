Pokemon Psyduck Yellow - ALPHA 0.4.6.0 - November 26, 2026

*** ALL FEATURES MAY NOT BE IN FINAL VERSION. ***
*** STILL IN THE LATE STAGES OF TESTING, AND COMPLETE APART FROM VISUAL POLISH. ***
If you run into any problem, please let us know by submitting an official Issue in the Issues section of this repository: https://github.com/Doctor-Pokemon/Psyduck-Yellow-Releases/issues

The Kanto campaign (Pallet Town to your first Hall of Fame) and the Johto postgame (to the second Hall of Fame) are built, plus crafting and Bill's Garden. It may still contain bugs.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Open the patching page: https://raw.githack.com/Doctor-Pokemon/Psyduck-Yellow-Releases/main/prototype-alpha0460/index.html
2. Choose your OWN, original, unmodified USA HeartGold .nds file (not another prototype).
3. Click "Create my playable NDS", wait for the checks to finish, then download the result ("Pokemon Psyduck Yellow ALPHA 0-4-6-0.nds") and load it in your DS emulator.
4. Start a NEW GAME. Saves from older versions do not work. Keep this build and its saves separate, and never reuse emulator save states between versions.
Nothing is uploaded; the patching happens in your browser and every step is hash-checked. Expected final SHA256: e1d59ce992e58bb3728534feb400828d152229d1bcc22f23aa7719d15f656040

GAME FEATURES
STORY AND REGIONS
- Pokémon Yellow's Kanto story retold inside Pokémon HeartGold: start with Psyduck, Togepi or Eevee, and meet Yellow's trainers, teams, wild Pokémon and dialogue on the way to Champion Blue and your first Hall of Fame.
- Jesse and James of Team Rocket battle you as real two-trainer double battles, with Yellow's teams, and say farewell at the Indigo Plateau later on.
- Kanto's dungeons are rebuilt from Yellow's maps with their puzzles: Mt. Moon, Rock Tunnel, the Pokémon Tower, the Rocket Hideout, Silph Co., the Pokémon Mansion, the Viridian Gym, Victory Road and the Safari Zone.
- After the first Hall of Fame the Johto postgame opens: the S.S. Aqua, Johto's eight Gyms (their teams grow with your Johto badges), the Johto League, Red and a second Hall of Fame. More special things are waiting to be discovered in Johto.
- NEW: Bill's Garden. After Bill gives you an Eevee in Johto, visit his Sea Cottage on Route 25 again: he leads you through a Secret Tunnel to his hidden garden, where Grass Pokémon from every region live.

POKÉMON
- A redesigned Eevee and Psyduck starter line, with new Eeveelutions and the Stellar-type Stellareon.
- Four new fossil Pokémon (Raptozolt, Dracosaur, Dunklevish and Arctosaur, art by @MACKSFAKEMON), revived at the Pewter Museum.
- Before your first Hall of Fame, Kanto's wild Pokémon are the original 151, in the places FireRed and LeafGreen use. Afterwards a second set of wild Pokémon spreads the whole National Pokédex over Kanto and Johto.
- Legendary Pokémon after the Hall of Fame: themed spots for many of them, a different legendary each day in the Embedded Tower with the Red Orb, and Rayquaza after the Jade Orb.
- The Form Trader swaps regional forms (Alolan, Galarian and Hisuian), the Linking Cord evolves every trade evolution, and the Pokémon League shop sells evolution items.
- Experience depends on the level of the Pokémon you defeat.

CRAFTING (NEW)
- Wild Pokémon sometimes drop a crafting material after a battle; every Pokémon family has its own (Feather, Bark, Spark, Fossil Fragment, Canine Fur and about fifty more). Headbutting a tree can shake Bark loose.
- Kirk, in the house on Route 2, gives you the Ball Craft Kit and teaches you to craft.
- The Apricorn Workbench crafts Poké Balls from materials in your Bag. The TM Machine crafts TMs from stored materials. Both stand in every Pokémon Center of a town with a Gym (Kanto and Johto) and in Cinnabar's Pokémon Center; Kirk's house has a Workbench too.
- TMs are single-use, as in the original Yellow, so crafting is how you get more.
- A MATERIALS menu moves materials between your Bag and the material storage and shows how many you have.
- The Black Belt at the Saffron Fighting Dojo sells Gems for some recipes.

KEY ITEMS
- Pokémon Box Link (from Bill in Johto): your PC Boxes from the Bag, anywhere: deposit, withdraw, move Pokémon and items, plus the MATERIALS menu.
- Silph Co. Field Kit: with the right Badge, Cut, Rock Smash, Strength, Surf, Rock Climb, Waterfall and Whirlpool work without a Pokémon that knows the move.
- Taxi Pager (from Lt. Surge): opens the Fly map from the Bag.
- Ball Craft Kit (from Kirk) and the Card Album.
- Form-change items from the Oak's Lab aide once you have caught the right Pokémon: Gracidea, Reveal Glass, DNA Splicers, Prison Bottle, N-Solarizer, N-Lunarizer and Reins of Unity (these last three fuse and split Necrozma and Calyrex with their partners).
- The Magnet Train Pass, a real Poké Flute for the sleeping Snorlax, and Yellow's story items.

COMFORT
- Gift Pokémon go to a PC Box when your party is full, and you can nickname gifts and revived fossils.
- Item Storage in every Pokémon Center PC, a shared Day Care, and Kanto Marts that share their stock.
- The Kanto Gym Leaders give Stellar Sandwich ingredients with their Badges, and Meowth hosts Pokémon Talk on the radio after the Jesse and James farewell.

WHAT IS NEW SINCE ALPHA 1.8
- Replaces ALPHA 1.8. Everything from ALPHA 1.8 is still here. All features may not be in final version.
- NEW: CRAFTING. Wild Pokémon sometimes drop crafting materials after a battle (Feather, Bark, Spark, Fossil Fragment, Canine Fur and about fifty more); they go into your Bag. Every Pokémon family has its own material.
- Kirk, in the house on Route 2, gives you the Ball Craft Kit and explains crafting. You can build Poké Balls from materials at the new Apricorn Workbench, which stands in every Pokémon Center of a town with a Gym (Kanto and Johto), in Cinnabar's Pokémon Center and in Kirk's house.
- The new TM Machine (in the same Pokémon Centers) crafts TMs from stored materials. TMs are single-use again, like in the original Yellow, so you can craft a replacement when you need one. Some recipes use materials that only appear after the first Hall of Fame.
- A MATERIALS menu (at the TM Machine, the Apricorn Workbench and in the Pokémon Box Link) moves materials between your Bag and the material storage, showing how many you have of each.
- Headbutting a tree can shake Bark loose. The Black Belt at the Saffron Fighting Dojo now sells Gems.
- Before your first Hall of Fame, wild Pokémon in Kanto are only the original 151, in the places FireRed and LeafGreen use. A rare Vileplume lives on Routes 14 and 15.
- Experience now depends on the level of the Pokémon you defeat, not on the level of your own Pokémon.
- NEW: BILL'S GARDEN. After Bill gives you the Eevee in Johto, visit his Sea Cottage on Route 25 again. Bill leads you through a Secret Tunnel to his hidden garden, where Grass Pokémon from every region live in the tall grass.
- Bill in Johto now really hands over the Pokémon Box Link (before, his scene ended early and the item was never given).
- Fixed several crashes on a real 3DS: one while walking during some scenes (for example the Pewter City guide), Bill's PC list and the Cerulean badge guide list, and a missing-picture crash. An Egg's summary page no longer shows the wrong picture.
- The save layout is unchanged.

WHAT HAS BEEN TESTED
The developer has played New Game through the Kanto league and first Hall of Fame, and the Johto league and second Hall of Fame, partly on a real 3DS. Crafting (drops, the Apricorn Workbench, the TM Machine and the MATERIALS menu) has been played on a real 3DS. Bill's Garden has been played through in an emulator; Bill's escort and the garden's final layout have only been checked in emulator tests. Every new script passes an automatic crash check in the emulator before release. Expect bugs, including possible softlocks.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Some of the newest lines (Kirk, the TM Machine, the Apricorn Workbench and the people in Bill's Garden) are first drafts.
- Some TM and Poké Ball recipes need materials that no Pokémon in Kanto drops before the first Hall of Fame; they become craftable later.
- While two Pokémon are fused, the partner is stored in the save and shows nowhere else: do not release, trade away or deposit the fused Pokémon before splitting it (the Box Link's GET PARTNER button returns a partner).
- Several Johto characters (not the Gym Leaders) still hand out HMs that you already own from Kanto.
- Many of the added Gen 5-9 items still show a "?" icon in the bag.
- The custom Eeveelutions do not follow you in the overworld yet (their follower art is not made). Their stats and moves, and those of the four fossil Pokémon, are first drafts.
- Catching the new legendary Pokémon, the weekly rollover of the daily encounter and the real Mew and Arceus conditions have not been tested end to end.
