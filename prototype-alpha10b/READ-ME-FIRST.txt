Pokemon Psyduck Yellow - ALPHA 1.0 (pre-release) - November 16, 2026

*** ALL FEATURES MAY NOT BE IN FINAL VERSION. ***
*** PRE-RELEASE: STILL IN THE LATE STAGES OF TESTING, AND COMPLETE APART FROM VISUAL POLISH. ***
If you run into any problem, please let us know by submitting an official Issue in the Issues section of this repository: https://github.com/Doctor-Pokemon/Psyduck-Yellow-Releases/issues

The Kanto campaign (Pallet Town to your first Hall of Fame) and the Johto postgame (to the second Hall of Fame) are built. This is a pre-release and may still contain bugs.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Open the patching page: https://raw.githack.com/Doctor-Pokemon/Psyduck-Yellow-Releases/main/prototype-alpha10b/index.html
2. Choose your OWN, original, unmodified USA HeartGold .nds file (not another prototype).
3. Click "Create my playable NDS", wait for the checks to finish, then download the result ("Pokemon Psyduck Yellow ALPHA 1-0.nds") and load it in your DS emulator.
4. Start a NEW GAME. Saves from older versions do not work. Keep this build and its saves separate, and never reuse emulator save states between versions.
Nothing is uploaded; the patching happens in your browser and every step is hash-checked. Expected final SHA256: 9badacca024907f3c401568347f0ec5699a6a10d0b0cd6878fe7123efd9b1b03

WHAT IS NEW SINCE ALPHA 0.2.2
- Replaces ALPHA 0.2.2. Everything from ALPHA 0.2.2 is still here. All features may not be in final version.
- Jesse and James of Team Rocket are real two-trainer double battles: at Mt. Moon after the fossil ambush, in the Celadon Rocket Hideout, in the Pokémon Tower, in a new scene on Silph Co.'s 11th floor, and in the Viridian Gym (where they stand in for Giovanni, with TM46 Thief as the reward). Their sprites, names and teams follow Yellow.
- A farewell scene with Jesse, James and Meowth now plays at the Indigo Plateau after you hold all sixteen Badges and before the second Hall of Fame. Afterwards the Meowth in the Goldenrod Radio Tower studio hosts Pokémon Talk, with its own intros, segments and a few special guests.
- Cinnabar Island is HeartGold's ruined island for the whole game, and after your first Hall of Fame Giovanni waits there. Blaine's Gym is back inside the Seafoam Islands cave.
- The Pewter Museum scientists revive fossils again: HeartGold's own scientist handles the Kanto fossils, and a second scientist revives Cover, Plume, Jaw and Sail Fossils and the Fossilized Bird, Fish, Drake and Dino pairs.
- More legendary and mythical Pokémon can be found in the Embedded Tower, Cresselia and Shaymin have their own spots, and the Pokédex Area page now shows where the added wild Pokémon live.
- The Johto Gym Leaders and Elite Four have new, tougher teams.
- The Celadon Department Store sells TM Trick Room, and HMs are no longer sold in shops.
- Fixed a red-screen crash at the Johto Hall of Fame on a real 3DS, and a field-menu bug that appeared after the Rocket farewell scene.
- Fixed the earlier known issues: the top screen turning black in the overworld, move descriptions cut off on the summary screen, the red-screen crashes in the Ecruteak Gym path and the Pokémon menu, and the Lavender Town building looking like the Radio Tower.
- The save layout is unchanged.

WHAT HAS BEEN TESTED
The developer has played New Game through the Kanto league and first Hall of Fame, and the Johto league and second Hall of Fame, partly on a real 3DS, and has seen the Rocket farewell scene, Pokémon Talk and Giovanni on Cinnabar. The museum fossil scientists, the legendary encounters and the other items listed under known issues have only had small emulator checks on prepared test saves and are not confirmed on hardware. Expect bugs, including possible softlocks.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Several Johto characters (not the Gym Leaders) still hand out HMs that you already own from Kanto.
- Many of the added Gen 5-9 items still show a "?" icon in the bag.
- Catching the new legendary Pokémon, the weekly rollover of the daily encounter and the real Mew and Arceus conditions (nearly the whole National Pokédex registered) have not been tested end to end.
- The Pewter City scenes with Steven and Latios, the Magnet Train ride after a real Hall of Fame and Rayquaza after the Jade Orb have only been checked in pieces on prepared test saves, not played through.
