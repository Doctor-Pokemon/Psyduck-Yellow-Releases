Pokemon Psyduck Yellow - ALPHA 0.2.2 (pre-release) - November 13, 2026

*** ALL FEATURES MAY NOT BE IN FINAL VERSION. ***
*** PRE-RELEASE: STILL IN THE LATE STAGES OF TESTING, BUT SO FAR IT SEEMS TO BE COMPLETE. ***
If you run into any problem, please let us know by submitting an official Issue in the Issues section of this repository: https://github.com/Doctor-Pokemon/Psyduck-Yellow-Releases/issues

The Kanto campaign (Pallet Town to your first Hall of Fame) is built, and the Johto postgame is in progress but UNFINISHED and has not been played through.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Open the patching page: https://raw.githack.com/Doctor-Pokemon/Psyduck-Yellow-Releases/main/prototype-alpha022b/index.html
2. Choose your OWN, original, unmodified USA HeartGold .nds file (not another prototype).
3. Click "Create my playable NDS", wait for the checks to finish, then download the result ("Pokemon Psyduck Yellow ALPHA 0-2-2.nds") and load it in your DS emulator.
4. Start a NEW GAME. Saves from older versions do not work. Keep this build and its saves separate, and never reuse emulator save states between versions.
Nothing is uploaded; the patching happens in your browser and every step is hash-checked. Expected final SHA256: 8730ada7c8c35e65c8939561a83a3132cd9ae7b827afc31067b6c11d3d969174

WHAT IS NEW SINCE ALPHA 0.1
- Replaces ALPHA 0.1. Everything from ALPHA 0.1 is still here. All features may not be in final version.
- The Silph Co. Field Kit now covers more: with the Kit in your bag and the right Badge, Surf, Rock Climb, Waterfall and Whirlpool work without a Pokémon that knows the move (Cut, Rock Smash and Strength already did). Its lines no longer name your Pokémon ("Field Kit used Strength!").
- Added a Taxi Pager key item: Lt. Surge gives it to you, once, with the Thunder Badge. Using it from the bag opens the Fly map and your lead Pokémon flies you there (a Corviknight shows in the Fly animation). Its texts are placeholders.
- The Magnet Train Pass can be earned: winning the Kanto League now switches the Power Plant on, then the Copycat in Saffron City (her Clefairy doll, found through the woman in the Vermilion Pokémon Fan Club) gives you the Pass.
- Steven Stone is back in the Pewter Museum and in Pewter City. The Hiker at the Embedded Tower entrance on Route 47 gives you an Enigma Stone; with it, the museum plays Steven's Enigma Stone scene, and afterwards Steven and Latios wait outside the museum in Pewter City. Latios can be battled (level 60 plus 5 for every Johto Badge) and caught. This chain has not been played through in order.
- The Vs. Recorder is back as a normal item, and the Card Album has its own key item.
- Fixed a softlock at the Olivine S.S. Aqua gate (a Professor Oak scene walked you out of the room) and one on the return ferry (talking to the sailor in the Vermilion port hall froze the game). Repaired eight NPC, sign and trigger scripts in six Kanto maps that ran another character's script.
- The save layout is unchanged.

WHAT HAS BEEN TESTED
The developer has played New Game through Lavender Town on earlier builds, partly on a real 3DS, and the Kanto league and first Hall of Fame on hardware. The Field Kit and Taxi Pager changes, the Magnet Train Pass, the Steven and Latios scenes and the other additions in this version have only had small emulator checks on prepared test saves and are not confirmed on hardware. Everything after Lavender and most of Johto has never been played through in one go.

KNOWN ISSUES
- The Pewter City scenes with Steven and Latios, the Magnet Train ride after a real Hall of Fame and Rayquaza after the Jade Orb have only been checked in pieces on prepared test saves, not played through.
- Catching the new legendary Pokémon, the weekly rollover of the daily encounter and the real Mew and Arceus conditions (nearly the whole National Pokédex registered) have not been tested end to end.
- Several Johto characters (not the Gym Leaders) still hand out HMs that you already own from Kanto.
- The top screen can turn black in the overworld (a save and reload fixes it).
- Some move descriptions on the summary screen's Moves page are cut off at the right edge.
- Two red-screen crashes were each reported once on a real 3DS and could not be reproduced: falling off the path on the way out of the Ecruteak Gym, and opening the Pokémon menu.
- Many of the added Gen 5-9 items still show a "?" icon in the bag.
- The Lavender Town building with the Pokémon Tower door still looks like the Radio Tower from outside.
- Johto is unfinished.
