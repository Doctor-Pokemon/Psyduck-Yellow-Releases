# Changelog

All notable changes to **Pokémon Psyduck Yellow**, an unofficial fan project that rebuilds Pokémon Yellow's Kanto campaign inside Pokémon HeartGold. Newest first. These are betas for testing; see the [Releases](../../releases) page for the downloads and the patching page.

## BETA 0.2.1.0 (prototype 0210) — November 11, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- Added Hisuian Forms to Form Trader NPC.

## BETA 0.2.0.0 (prototype 0200) — November 11, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- **Johto postgame, further along:** the first Johto scenes (Elm's lab, the New Bark Town and Route 29 gate scenes) were repaired; a Johto Gym Leader is always in the gym until you have beaten him, and the postgame weekday schedule applies afterwards; Johto Gym Leaders' teams scale with your Johto badges; the S.S. Aqua ferry and its lost-granddaughter quest follow HeartGold's own flow.
- **HMs from the Kanto gyms:** Brock gives Rock Smash, Misty Waterfall, Surge Fly, Blaine Rock Climb and Giovanni Whirlpool; the S.S. Anne captain gives Cut; Sabrina still gives Strength and Janine Surf. Each leader says what the HM does in the field and which Badge unlocks it.
- **Silph Co. Field Kit:** after the Master Ball the Silph Co. President also gives you a Field Kit. With the right Badge it lets you clear Cut trees, Rock Smash rocks and Strength boulders without a Pokémon that knows the move.
- **Bag descriptions:** items show real descriptions again (HeartGold's own text for the original items, new text for 52 of the added items).
- **National Dex after your first Hall of Fame:** Kanto and Johto then use a second set of wild Pokémon tables. Hundreds of species the game never handed out now turn up in the rarer slots of grass, caves and water (legendary-class Pokémon are held back for later events). Before the first Hall of Fame nothing changes.
- **Evolutions:** the Linking Cord now evolves every Pokémon that evolves by trade. Spritzee needs a held Sachet and Swirlix a held Whipped Dream, as in the games, and Kubfu can use its Scrolls.
- **Pokémon League shop:** after your first Hall of Fame the clerk in the Pokémon League lobby also sells the evolution items nothing else gives out (the stones, Linking Cord, Whipped Dream, Sachet, Apples, Pots, Galarica items, Black Augurite, Peat Block, Scrolls, Armors and Metal Alloy).
- **Form Trader:** a new NPC in the Johto Safari Zone Gate swaps a Pokémon for its Alolan or Galarian form (34 forms). Its stats, ability and moves follow the new form. The swap is one way.
- **3DS:** a red-screen crash right before Morty's battle was worked around (confirmed on a real 3DS).
- Checked mostly with small emulator tests on prepared saves; only some Johto parts are confirmed on a real 3DS.

## BETA 0.1.6.9 (prototype 0169) — November 4, 2026
- **Strength and Surf can be obtained.** Sabrina gives HM04 Strength after the Marsh Badge and Janine (Fuchsia Gym) gives HM03 Surf after your first win there, each once. Strength now works with the Plain Badge or the Marsh Badge.
- **Fuchsia City:** Officer Jenny closes the Route 15 and Route 18 gatehouses until you hold the Marsh Badge. The Warden's granddaughter gives you his GOLD TEETH; the Warden rewards you with NUGGETS and is only at home in the day and evening. A full bag at his first NUGGET no longer leaves him mumbling forever.
- **Route 12:** a sleeping Snorlax now appears from the start of the game and wakes to the Poké Flute, which is now a real key item (Key Items pocket, cannot be thrown away, new icon). It is played when you talk to the Snorlax; there is no USE button in the bag yet.
- **Celadon Game Corner:** the slot machines say "This machine is out of order." instead of hanging the game.
- **Bag icons:** the Running Shoes and the Silph Scope have real icons instead of the "?" placeholder.
- Checked only with small emulator tests on prepared saves; not confirmed on a real 3DS yet.

## BETA 0.1.6.8 (prototype 0168) — November 3, 2026
- A Route 7 / Route 8 shortcut opens the way from Lavender Town to Celadon City.
- Celadon: the Game Corner can be entered from the street; Mr. Game's room moved behind the red door next to it.
- Rocket Hideout: the spinner tiles show arrows that point the way they push you.
- Saffron Fighting Dojo: the man at the back battles you as a Black Belt; after you win you choose Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan.
- Lavender Town: the Pokémon Tower door and sign follow Yellow. Pewter City: Brock's escort walks faster. Both Kanto Mart clerks sell. Vermilion Gym sign updated.

Earlier prototypes are listed on the [Releases](../../releases) page. The save layout has not changed between these betas; start a **New Game** for each version.
