# Changelog

All notable changes to **Pokémon Psyduck Yellow**, an unofficial fan project that rebuilds Pokémon Yellow's Kanto campaign inside Pokémon HeartGold. Newest first. These are betas for testing; see the [Releases](../../releases) page for the downloads and the patching page.

## BETA 0.1.6.9 (prototype 0169) — November 4, 2026
- **Strength and Surf can be obtained.** Sabrina gives HM04 Strength after the Marsh Badge and Janine (Fuchsia Gym) gives HM03 Surf after your first win there, each once. Strength now works with the Plain Badge or the Marsh Badge.
- **Fuchsia City:** Officer Jenny closes the Route 15 and Route 18 gatehouses until you hold the Marsh Badge. The Warden's granddaughter gives you his GOLD TEETH; the Warden rewards you with NUGGETS and is only at home in the day and evening. A full bag at his first NUGGET no longer leaves him mumbling forever.
- **Route 12:** a sleeping Snorlax now appears from the start of the game and wakes to the Poké Flute, which is now a real key item (Key Items pocket, cannot be thrown away, new icon). It is played when you talk to the Snorlax; there is no USE button in the bag yet.
- **Celadon Game Corner:** the slot machines say "This machine is out of order." instead of hanging the game.
- **Bag icons:** the Running Shoes and the Silph Scope have real icons instead of the "?" placeholder.
- Checked only with small emulator tests on prepared saves; not confirmed on a real 3DS yet.

## BETA 0.1.6.8 (prototype 0168) — November 3, 2026
- A Route 7 / Route 8 shortcut opens the way from Lavender Town to Celadon City.
- Celadon: the Game Corner can be entered from the street; Mr. Game's room moved behind the red door next to it.
- Rocket Hideout: the spinner tiles show arrows that point the way they push you.
- Saffron Fighting Dojo: the man at the back battles you as a Black Belt; after you win you choose Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan.
- Lavender Town: the Pokémon Tower door and sign follow Yellow. Pewter City: Brock's escort walks faster. Both Kanto Mart clerks sell. Vermilion Gym sign updated.

Earlier prototypes are listed on the [Releases](../../releases) page. The save layout has not changed between these betas; start a **New Game** for each version.
