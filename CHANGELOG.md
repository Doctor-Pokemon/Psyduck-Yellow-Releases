# Changelog

All notable changes to **Pokémon Psyduck Yellow**, an unofficial fan project that rebuilds Pokémon Yellow's Kanto campaign inside Pokémon HeartGold. Newest first. These are betas for testing; see the [Releases](../../releases) page for the downloads and the patching page.

## ALPHA 1.7 (pre-release) — November 22, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- The Pokémon Box Link now opens a menu: Deposit, Withdraw, Move Pokémon, Move Items, and it returns to the menu after each box screen (before, it only opened the deposit screen).
- The Gracidea now also turns a Sky Forme Shaymin back into its Land Forme (before, it only worked one way).
- The four new fossil Pokémon (Raptozolt, Dracosaur, Dunklevish, Arctosaur) no longer evolve (they could be evolved into Eevee's family by mistake).
- Fusing with the N-Solarizer, N-Lunarizer or Reins of Unity now teaches the fused form its signature move (Sunsteel Strike, Moongeist Beam, Glacial Lance, Astral Barrage); splitting takes it away again (Necrozma gets Photon Geyser back).

## ALPHA 1.6 (pre-release) — November 22, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- The game's second copies of the N-Solarizer, N-Lunarizer and Reins of Unity (the ones listed as "separate" items, which a save that was edited by hand can hold) now work exactly like the first ones: fuse and split, with the same description.

## ALPHA 1.5 (pre-release) — November 22, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- The **N-Solarizer**, **N-Lunarizer** and **Reins of Unity** now work, like the DNA Splicers do for Kyurem. From the Bag, use the N-Solarizer on a Necrozma while a Solgaleo is in your party, the N-Lunarizer on a Necrozma with a Lunala, or the Reins of Unity on a Calyrex with a Glastrier or Spectrier: the two fuse into one Pokémon and the partner is kept safe in your save. Use the same item on the fused Pokémon (with a free party slot) to split them again and get the partner back.
- The Oak's Lab aide now also hands out the N-Solarizer (Necrozma and Solgaleo caught), the N-Lunarizer (Necrozma and Lunala caught) and the Reins of Unity (Calyrex and Glastrier or Spectrier caught), once each, like the other form-change items.
- The DNA Splicers, Reveal Glass, Prison Bottle, N-Solarizer, N-Lunarizer and Reins of Unity now have a description in the Bag (first drafts).

## ALPHA 1.4 (pre-release) — November 22, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- The **Prison Bottle** now works: use it from the Bag on a Hoopa and pick it in the party menu to switch it between its Confined and Unbound forms, as the Reveal Glass does for the Forces of Nature.
- The form-change key items can now be found in the game. Talk to the Oak's Lab aide after you have caught the Pokémon an item belongs to and the aide hands it over once, if it is not in your Bag yet: Gracidea (Shaymin), Reveal Glass (Tornadus, Thundurus or Landorus), DNA Splicers (Kyurem) and Prison Bottle (Hoopa).

## ALPHA 1.3 (pre-release) — November 22, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- Fixed a crash on a real 3DS in some battles with a legendary Pokémon's special in-battle form change: the small emblem next to the battle menu is no longer shown for it (the form change itself still works).
- New key item: the **Pokémon Box Link**. Use it from the Bag, anywhere, to open your PC Boxes. The Oak's Lab aide gives it to you together with the Card Album.
- The Gracidea and the Prison Bottle no longer open the Taxi Pager's flight page when you use them from the Bag. The Gracidea works on Shaymin again; the Prison Bottle opens the party menu for now, and its Hoopa form change follows in a later version.

## ALPHA 1.2 (pre-release) — November 22, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- Fixed a crash on a real 3DS when you step from Cinnabar Island onto Route 20 (the same kind of entry script in a few other maps, mostly Gym and city maps, was changed the same way).
- Pokémon you receive as gifts now go to a PC box when your party is full, with a message after the usual "received" line. Gifts and revived fossils also ask whether you want to give the new Pokémon a nickname.
- The second Pewter Museum scientist now lets you choose which fossil to revive, with a menu of what you can actually revive, like the first scientist.
- Four new fossil Pokémon replace the Galar fossil revivals: the Fossilized Bird revives Raptozolt, the Fossilized Drake Dracosaur, the Fossilized Fish Dunklevish and the Fossilized Dino Arctosaur (new Pokédex pages 1036-1039, new art and icons). Their art was drawn by @MACKSFAKEMON, with thanks.
- Rayquaza can no longer learn Dragon Ascent by levelling up or from a Move Reminder: Grandma Wilma in Blackthorn City now teaches it, to any Rayquaza in your party that does not know it. The Rayquaza in the Embedded Tower already knows Dragon Ascent.

## ALPHA 1.1 (pre-release) — November 18, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- The Kanto Gym Leaders now give out the ingredients for the Stellar Sandwich, one each with their badge: Brock and Surge give Canari Bread, Misty Salty Herba Mystica, Erika Sweet Herba Mystica, Janine Sour Herba Mystica, Sabrina the Stellar Tera Shard, Blaine Spicy Herba Mystica, and Jesse and James in the Viridian Gym the Bitter Herba Mystica.
- The Player's PC in every Pokémon Center now has the same Item Storage as the PC in your bedroom.
- More later-game items now have proper bag icons, including the Herba Mystica, the Stellar Tera Shard, Canari Bread, the Tera Shards, the Hyper berries and the Mochi. Item pictures come from the Serebii.net Item Dex and from PKHeX's item artwork (PKHeX is GPLv3; the credits are in the source).
- Fixed a freeze when you answered No to "Would you like to use another Repel?" (it showed up in the Pal Park, but happened anywhere).
- Fixed Technical Machines that could not be picked up while you already held the same one: you can now pick up a second copy.

## ALPHA 1.0 (pre-release) — November 16, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- Jesse and James of Team Rocket are real two-trainer double battles: at Mt. Moon after the fossil ambush, in the Celadon Rocket Hideout, in the Pokémon Tower, in a new scene on Silph Co.'s 11th floor, and in the Viridian Gym (where they stand in for Giovanni, with TM46 Thief as the reward). Their sprites, names and teams follow Yellow.
- A farewell scene with Jesse, James and Meowth now plays at the Indigo Plateau after you hold all sixteen Badges and before the second Hall of Fame. Afterwards the Meowth in the Goldenrod Radio Tower studio hosts Pokémon Talk, with its own intros, segments and a few special guests.
- Cinnabar Island is HeartGold's ruined island for the whole game, and after your first Hall of Fame Giovanni waits there. Blaine's Gym is back inside the Seafoam Islands cave.
- The Pewter Museum scientists revive fossils again: HeartGold's own scientist handles the Kanto fossils, and a second scientist revives Cover, Plume, Jaw and Sail Fossils and the Fossilized Bird, Fish, Drake and Dino pairs.
- More legendary and mythical Pokémon can be found in the Embedded Tower, Cresselia and Shaymin have their own spots, and the Pokédex Area page now shows where the added wild Pokémon live.
- The Johto Gym Leaders and Elite Four have new, tougher teams.
- The Celadon Department Store sells TM Trick Room, and HMs are no longer sold in shops.
- Fixed a red-screen crash at the Johto Hall of Fame on a real 3DS, and a field-menu bug that appeared after the Rocket farewell scene.
- Fixed the earlier known issues: the top screen turning black in the overworld, move descriptions cut off on the summary screen, the red-screen crashes in the Ecruteak Gym path and the Pokémon menu, and the Lavender Town building looking like the Radio Tower.

## ALPHA 0.2.2 (pre-release) — November 13, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- The Silph Co. Field Kit now covers more: with the Kit in your bag and the right Badge, Surf, Rock Climb, Waterfall and Whirlpool work without a Pokémon that knows the move (Cut, Rock Smash and Strength already did). Its lines no longer name your Pokémon ("Field Kit used Strength!").
- Added a Taxi Pager key item: Lt. Surge gives it to you, once, with the Thunder Badge. Using it from the bag opens the Fly map and your lead Pokémon flies you there (a Corviknight shows in the Fly animation). Its texts are placeholders.
- The Magnet Train Pass can be earned: winning the Kanto League now switches the Power Plant on, then the Copycat in Saffron City (her Clefairy doll, found through the woman in the Vermilion Pokémon Fan Club) gives you the Pass.
- Steven Stone is back in the Pewter Museum and in Pewter City. The Hiker at the Embedded Tower entrance on Route 47 gives you an Enigma Stone; with it, the museum plays Steven's Enigma Stone scene, and afterwards Steven and Latios wait outside the museum in Pewter City. Latios can be battled (level 60 plus 5 for every Johto Badge) and caught. This chain has not been played through in order.
- The Vs. Recorder is back as a normal item, and the Card Album has its own key item.
- Fixed a softlock at the Olivine S.S. Aqua gate (a Professor Oak scene walked you out of the room) and one on the return ferry (talking to the sailor in the Vermilion port hall froze the game). Repaired eight NPC, sign and trigger scripts in six Kanto maps that ran another character's script.

## ALPHA 0.1 (pre-release) — November 12, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- Legendary Pokémon can now be obtained. After your first Hall of Fame, Celebi waits at the Ilex Forest shrine, Regirock in Union Cave, Regice on the deepest floor of the Ice Path, Registeel in Mt. Mortar, Uxie outside the Ruins of Alph, Mesprit at the Lake of Rage and Azelf in Union Cave, each once you own enough Johto badges and only while that species is not yet registered as caught.
- Mr. Pokémon now gives both the Blue Orb and the Red Orb. With the Red Orb in your bag, the orb in the Embedded Tower (the chamber behind the lower door on Route 47) summons one legendary Pokémon per day (a new one each weekday): it appears as its own follower sprite, walks up to you, and the battle starts when it touches you. Mew and Arceus can only appear once nearly all other Pokémon are registered as caught.
- Added a Card Album key item with Gym Leader and Elite Four cards (Kanto and Johto) in three rarities. The card texts are placeholders.
- Lorelei and Agatha have their own battle and overworld sprites, and the Kanto Elite Four speak their Pokémon Yellow lines.
- Fixed a freeze: the battle against Lorelei (and the one against Agatha) in the Kanto Elite Four never started on emulators and crashed on a 3DS.
- Fixed the abilities of the new Eeveelutions and of Novareon: they showed impossible ability numbers and the wrong second and hidden abilities; they now have their intended abilities.
- The Jade Orb can be obtained again: after you have caught both Kyogre and Groudon in the Embedded Tower, Professor Oak gives it to you, and it now opens the way to Rayquaza. The orb in the Embedded Tower now works in all three chambers.
- The player's PC Item Storage now starts empty on a New Game (no starting items, evolution items or charms), and Fly can no longer be used until you have the Thunder Badge (the Fly HM still comes from Lt. Surge).
- Trainers' Pokémon no longer come out of Cherish Balls, and the Johto Gym Leaders no longer mention badges unlocking HM field moves (the HMs now come from the Kanto leaders).

## BETA 0.2.0.0 (prototype 0200) — November 11, 2026
**All features may not be in final version.**
- **Johto postgame, further along:** the first Johto scenes (Elm's lab, the New Bark Town and Route 29 gate scenes) were repaired; a Johto Gym Leader is always in the gym until you have beaten him, and the postgame weekday schedule applies afterwards; Johto Gym Leaders' teams scale with your Johto badges; the S.S. Aqua ferry and its lost-granddaughter quest follow HeartGold's own flow.
- **HMs from the Kanto gyms:** Brock gives Rock Smash, Misty Waterfall, Surge Fly, Blaine Rock Climb and Giovanni Whirlpool; the S.S. Anne captain gives Cut; Sabrina still gives Strength and Janine Surf. Each leader says what the HM does in the field and which Badge unlocks it.
- **Silph Co. Field Kit:** after the Master Ball the Silph Co. President also gives you a Field Kit. With the right Badge it lets you clear Cut trees, Rock Smash rocks and Strength boulders without a Pokémon that knows the move.
- **Bag descriptions:** items show real descriptions again (HeartGold's own text for the original items, new text for 52 of the added items).
- **National Dex after your first Hall of Fame:** Kanto and Johto then use a second set of wild Pokémon tables. Hundreds of species the game never handed out now turn up in the rarer slots of grass, caves and water (legendary-class Pokémon are held back for later events). Before the first Hall of Fame nothing changes.
- **Evolutions:** the Linking Cord now evolves every Pokémon that evolves by trade. Spritzee needs a held Sachet and Swirlix a held Whipped Dream, as in the games, and Kubfu can use its Scrolls.
- **Pokémon League shop:** after your first Hall of Fame the clerk in the Pokémon League lobby also sells the evolution items nothing else gives out (the stones, Linking Cord, Whipped Dream, Sachet, Apples, Pots, Galarica items, Black Augurite, Peat Block, Scrolls, Armors and Metal Alloy).
- **Form Trader:** a new NPC in the Johto Safari Zone Gate swaps a Pokémon for its Alolan or Galarian form (34 forms). Its stats, ability and moves follow the new form. The swap is one way.
- **3DS:** a red-screen crash right before Morty's battle was worked around (confirmed on a real 3DS).
- Checked mostly with small emulator tests on prepared saves; only some Johto parts are confirmed on a real 3DS.

## BETA 0.1.6.9 (prototype 0169) — November 4, 2026
- **Strength and Surf can be obtained.** Sabrina gives HM04 Strength after the Marsh Badge and Janine (Fuchsia Gym) gives HM03 Surf after your first win there, each once. Strength now works with the Plain Badge or the Marsh Badge.
- **Fuchsia City:** Officer Jenny closes the Route 15 and Route 18 gatehouses until you hold the Marsh Badge. The Warden's granddaughter gives you his GOLD TEETH; the Warden rewards you with NUGGETS and is only at home in the day and evening. A full bag at his first NUGGET no longer leaves him mumbling forever.
- **Route 12:** a sleeping Snorlax now appears from the start of the game and wakes to the Poké Flute, which is now a real key item (Key Items pocket, cannot be thrown away, new icon). It is played when you talk to the Snorlax; there is no USE button in the bag yet.
- **Celadon Game Corner:** the slot machines say "This machine is out of order." instead of hanging the game.
- **Bag icons:** the Running Shoes and the Silph Scope have real icons instead of the "?" placeholder.
- Checked only with small emulator tests on prepared saves; not confirmed on a real 3DS yet.

## BETA 0.1.6.8 (prototype 0168) — November 3, 2026
- A Route 7 / Route 8 shortcut opens the way from Lavender Town to Celadon City.
- Celadon: the Game Corner can be entered from the street; Mr. Game's room moved behind the red door next to it.
- Rocket Hideout: the spinner tiles show arrows that point the way they push you.
- Saffron Fighting Dojo: the man at the back battles you as a Black Belt; after you win you choose Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan.
- Lavender Town: the Pokémon Tower door and sign follow Yellow. Pewter City: Brock's escort walks faster. Both Kanto Mart clerks sell. Vermilion Gym sign updated.

Earlier prototypes are listed on the [Releases](../../releases) page. The save layout has not changed between these betas; start a **New Game** for each version.
